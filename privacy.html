Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Short version

Wayfare shows live transit departures. It has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no ads. If you choose to share your location, it is used inside your browser to find the nearest stop and estimate a walk, and it is never sent to us. Your last-used station and settings are saved on your own device only.

Who runs this

Wayfare is operated by Huzaim Malik, based in Milton, Ontario, Canada. Contact: huzaimm01@gmail.com. This policy is written with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) in mind.

What happens to your data

ItemWhat we do with it
Your locationOnly requested after you tap a button such as "Use my location" or "Find my nearest stop". Your browser asks for permission first. The coordinates are used on your device to sort stops by distance and estimate walking time. They are not sent to our server, not stored, and not included in any request to a transit agency. You can withdraw permission at any time in your browser or device settings.
Saved settingsYour browser's local storage keeps one entry named wf_v1 on your device: the agency, line, station or stop, route, direction and colour preference you last chose. It never leaves your device. Clear your site data to remove it.
Requests for departuresWhen you view a board, your browser asks our proxy for departures using a station code, stop ID or route number (for example "Union" or "route 504"). We do not ask for, receive or store your name, account, device ID or coordinates.
Your IP addressAny website receives the IP address of the device that connects to it. Our proxy runs on Cloudflare Workers. We do not log, store or analyse IP addresses and have turned off Worker request logging. Cloudflare, as our infrastructure provider, may process IP addresses and technical data under its own privacy policy.
Cookies and trackersNone. No analytics, advertising, fingerprinting or social media widgets.

Why we handle this information

We collect nothing that identifies you, and we use the minimum needed to show departures. Location is used only with your consent, given when you tap the button and allow the browser prompt. You can use every part of Wayfare except "nearest" and automatic walking time without sharing location.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent information, and we have none about you to share beyond what the providers above handle technically. We could be required to disclose information if the law compels us, but we hold no records that identify individual users.

How long we keep things

We keep no personal information. Settings on your device stay until you clear them. Cached transit data expires automatically.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. The Metrolinx API key is stored as a secret on Cloudflare and never sent to your browser. The proxy only accepts requests from this website's origin and validates every input before using it. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee that it is.

Where data is processed

GitHub, Cloudflare and Google operate globally, including in the United States. Technical data such as your IP address may be handled outside Canada and may be accessible to authorities in those countries under their laws.

Your choices and rights

Under PIPEDA you may ask whether we hold personal information about you, ask to see or correct it, and withdraw consent. Because we do not keep identifiable records, we expect the answer to be that we hold none, but write to huzaimm01@gmail.com and we will respond. If you are in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, the same limited handling described above applies, and you may also complain to your local data protection authority. In Canada you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

Wayfare is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Accuracy and independence

Wayfare is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the Toronto Transit Commission, Metrolinx, GO Transit, UP Express or any other agency. TTC, GO Transit, Metrolinx and UP Express names and marks belong to their owners and are used only to say which service a departure is for.

Departure times are predictions supplied by third parties. They can be late, early, missing or wrong, and walking times are estimates from straight-line distance. Do not rely on Wayfare where a missed or mistimed trip could cause harm. The service is provided as is, without warranties, and to the extent the law allows we are not liable for losses from using it.
